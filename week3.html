📝 What is an Inquiry Letter?

A letter written to request information about products, services, internships, scholarships, or job vacancies. May also be done via email or interview. All modes require correctness, clarity, conciseness, and courtesy.

📋 Letter of Inquiry - Basic Parts

• Heading • Inside Address • Salutation • Body (3 paragraphs) • Complimentary Close • Signature

Body: 1st para = background & purpose | Middle = specific info needed | Final = expectation & thanks

📄 Pure Block Format

📧 Email Inquiries

  • Be courteous and use positive tone
  • Keep concise and clear
  • Proofread before sending
  • Use a descriptive subject line

🎤 Interview as Inquiry

Before

  • Make appointment
  • Prepare questions

During

  • Be punctual
  • Speak clearly
  • Take notes
  • Thank interviewee

Parts

  • Opening (rapport)
  • Body (questions)
  • Conclusion (thanks)
